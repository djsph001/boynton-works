## What you get

A focused review of one repository or one defined problem.

- 3–5 verified findings
- Obvious security risks within the reviewed scope
- A clear recommendation: solid, needs fixes, or needs deeper review
- Plain-language summary you can share with your developer or contractor

## Who this is for

Small business owners who had software built and are not sure what they received.
Independent professionals with a project that has grown messy. Anyone inheriting
a codebase they did not write.

## What this is not

This is not a comprehensive forensic or security audit. It is a narrow,
independent look at a specific concern.

If your situation needs a deeper review — full architecture analysis, test
verification, claims validation — that is a separate professional service
with a different scope and price. I will tell you plainly if that is what
you need.

## How it works

1. You describe the problem or share the repository.
2. I review the code and verify the findings.
3. You receive a written report within 7 business days.
4. We can meet or call to go through it if you want.

## Pricing

Flat fee: $250–$500, depending on scope. A free 30-minute look helps determine
the right scope before you commit.
